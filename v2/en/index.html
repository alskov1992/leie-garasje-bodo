About the garage

Frequently asked questions

Can I rent for one day?

No. The minimum rental is one week. That lets us keep the equipment in good condition and prices low.

What if something breaks?

Call us and come by. We replace or repair it. Insurance is included; the excess in case of damage is [AMOUNT].

Do I need a boating licence?

Yes, for boats with an engine over 25 hp or longer than 8 metres, if you were born after 1 January 1980. The Norwegian licence is called båtførerbevis; many foreign licences are accepted.

How do I pay road tolls?

The car has an AutoPASS tag. Toll passages during the rental are invoiced to you afterwards.

Can I cancel?

Free of charge up to [NUMBER] days before pick-up. The money goes back to your card.

Do you deliver?

No. Everything is picked up and returned at [ADDRESS]. That keeps prices down.

Can I rent as a visitor to Norway?

Yes. You need a valid passport and a driving licence valid in Norway. Payment by card if you don't have Vipps.
